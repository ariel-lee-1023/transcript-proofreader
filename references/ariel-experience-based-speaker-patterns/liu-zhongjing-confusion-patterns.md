## Purpose & Strict Usage Rules

This table supplies candidate readings reported in Ariel Lee's work with Liu Zhongjing transcripts. It includes identity spellings, plausible sound confusions, and longer contextual reconstructions. These are different kinds of evidence. Political descriptions below summarize terminology associated with the speaker's discourse, not the proofreader's factual judgment or endorsement.

**Critical Rules (from transcript-proofreader core principles):**
- Use **only** when a trigger condition is met (e.g., phonetic similarity + abrupt semantic break, first occurrence of name-like string, known confusion pattern match, or context of warlord-era / class/order / 诸夏 theory / military-industrial / geopolitical strategy discussion).
- **Never override** the actual transcript content or speaker intent. If the transcript consistently uses a non-standard variant, respect it and document the choice in Entity Memory.
- Always cross-check against running Entity Memory, immediate surrounding sentences, and (when first occurrence or uncertain) perform tool-based verification (web_search / browse_page).
- If confidence is low after reasoning, preserve original and explicitly mark `存疑` in the modification notes.
- This table does **not** replace the Phonetic Reasoning Protocol or Default Verification Rule in the skill.

## Evidence gates before using a row

- Retain the unchanged reading as a candidate. A company topic does not exclude literal glass, and a discussion of Chinese provinces can still mention the Netherlands.
- Compare original and candidate pronunciations separately; several legacy Pinyin cells name only the proposed reading. They are not transcriptions of audio heard in this run. A semantic match does not establish acoustic similarity.
- Rows **37, 38, 41 and 43** include expansions, deletion of possible information, or broad semantic reconstruction. In particular, “冒绑在我的手里” → “把责任推到我身上” is an interpretive paraphrase, not a homophone correction. Treat these as possible meanings to query unless recording evidence or explicit user confirmation resolves the wording. Do not add them as recovered speech from topic alone.
- Identity rows (the same text in both columns) are spelling reminders, not errors. Negation, modality, names and evaluative labels require particular care. Do not choose a derogatory term merely because it suits a presumed worldview.
- Preserve uncertainty where a competing reading remains plausible. Report the row number, original wording, chosen/retained form and actual evidence in editing notes. A lookup establishes spelling or usage, not what was spoken.

## Additional Core 姨学 / Liu Zhongjing Terminology (Standard Forms)

These are the experience compilation's terminology cues and glosses, not independently verified claims about every source or occurrence. They describe usage within the associated discourse. Register a term in Entity Memory only when the current occurrence is confirmed; keep an unfamiliar or unsupported gloss provisional.

- **诸夏** — Decentralized, organic civilized polities / confederation model (idealized pre-Qin or alternative to Qin-style centralized empire). Key positive concept in national invention and order theory.
- **费拉** — De-civilized population characterized by loss of self-organizing capacity, low generalized trust, and parasitism on pre-existing order structures. Diagnostic (not purely pejorative) term for post-collapse or empire-degraded social state.
- **大洪水** — Metaphor for civilizational collapse, chaotic reset, and violent transitional period (dynastic cycle extreme, mass violence, breakdown of order). Often linked to 张献忠-style violence.
- **张献忠** (or 张献忠状态) — Archetype / metaphor for extreme chaotic violence, societal cannibalism, and pre-order-building warlordism (historical late-Ming figure used as type).
- **武德** — Martial virtue / organized capacity for disciplined violence and self-defense; positive prerequisite for nation-building and sustainable order production in Liu's framework.
- **自组织 / 自发秩序** — Spontaneous / bottom-up order creation (Hayek-influenced); contrasted with top-down imperial administration.
- **瓦房店化** — (If encountered) Degradation into low-quality imitation or shanzhai-style copying of institutions/technology (place-name derived term).
- **做题家** — Narrowly meritocratic exam-takers optimized for test performance but lacking broader order-building or 武德 capacity; often contrasted with productive or martial classes.
- **德匹下** — (Secondary term) Related to moral or civilizational decline metrics in Liu's analysis.

## Quick Reference: When to Trigger Verification vs Table

- First occurrence of a proper name, institution or technical term → verify when its written form is genuinely ambiguous or consequential. Mere novelty does not require a search.
- Clear phonetic/orthographic match to a row above + semantic awkwardness or topic match → consider table correction, but still verify if first time or low confidence.
- Consistent speaker usage of variant → respect and note in Entity Memory Update; do not "correct" to table form.
